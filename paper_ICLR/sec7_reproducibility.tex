\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

The model, the gate and the penalty are specified in Section~\ref{sec:method_model}--\ref{sec:xcov}, and the probe protocol --- frozen encoder, group-wise splits, non-overlapping evaluation windows, a minimum context length --- at the end of Section~\ref{sec:method_model}. The synthetic generator is specified completely in Section~\ref{sec:synthetic} and needs no download; the two real datasets are public \citep{wagner2020, reyesortiz2016, hapt2015}, and their preparation is in Section~\ref{sec:data}. The values we set by hand rather than derive are named as such in Section~\ref{sec:limits}. Every experiment writes a JSON file, and every table and every figure number in this paper is regenerated from those files by a script rather than transcribed, so a table and the sentence beside it cannot drift apart; the design ablations of Appendix~\ref{app:design} and the term decomposition of Appendix~\ref{app:probe} reuse stored checkpoints and need no retraining.
